LLMP Priority Hierarchy

🏰 Disneyland Priority Tiers

Book Early
Tier 1
Space Mountain & Indiana Jones Adventure:

Return slots push into afternoon very fast. Book Indy or Space 1st at 7:35 AM.

Tier 2
Matterhorn Bobsleds & Big Thunder Mountain:

Matterhorn breaks down occasionally; Big Thunder has high capacity, quick turnaround.

Tier 3
Pirates of the Caribbean (Standby only), Haunted Mansion:

Note: Pirates has NO Lightning Lane (rope drop standby!). HM uses virtual queue/LL when open.

🎡 DCA Priority Tiers

Stack for 11 AM
Tier 1
Guardians of the Galaxy: Mission BREAKOUT!

Highest DCA demand. Book for 11:30 AM – 1:00 PM while sitting in Disneyland.

Tier 2
Soarin' Around the World & Incredicoaster:

Great afternoon fillers. Single Rider is also extremely rapid for Incredicoaster.

Single Pass
Radiator Springs Racers ($21-$26 Single Pass):

NOT included in Multi Pass! Use Single Rider line (usually ~15 mins) to save money.
